About us

We are a team of passionate food lovers dedicated to bringing you the best culinary experiences. Our mission is to serve delicious, high-quality meals that satisfy your cravings and leave your wanting more

Who we are

Real food, Real love

Since 1983, FoodBuka has been about more than food it's about bold Nigerian flavors, tradition, and soulful nourishment.We began with a simple mission: to serve authentic, home-cooked Nigerian meals to busy city professionals and families who wanted real comfort food without the wait.

FoodBuka is more than just food, it's a way to relive special moments. From smoky Jollof rice to soft pounded yam and our unique Ofada rice, every dish takes you back to those warm Sundays with family and joyful village celebrations. Each meal is a proud reminder of Nigeria's rich culinary heritage.
